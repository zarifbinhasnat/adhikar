Abstract

This report documents, step by step, how the Adhikar legal-information app was extended with a question-answering system that runs entirely on the presenter's laptop. No question, document or answer leaves the machine. For every engineering step we record what was done, which alternatives were considered, the decision taken and the reasoning behind it, and the risks that remain. The report is regenerated from the repository, so its corpus tables, commit history and evaluation figures are the actual state of the code. Decisions that depended on measurement were settled by the benchmark in Section 9.

1Executive summary

Adhikar helps people in Bangladesh understand their legal rights, particularly in encounters with the police. This work adds a question-answering feature. A user asks a question in Bangla, English or romanised Bangla ("Banglish") and receives an answer that quotes and cites the exact provision of Bangladeshi law it relies on.

Three decisions define the work:

  1. All inference is local. The language model, the embedding model and the search index run on an Apple M4 MacBook Air with 16 GB of unified memory. The iPhone is only a display and input device, and it reaches the laptop over a private Wi-Fi link. This replaces the cloud design in the earlier RAG_ARCHITECTURE.md (see D1).
  2. Answers are grounded, not generated from memory. A 7–8B model does not reliably know Bangladeshi law. It is used only to explain provisions that the retrieval system has found, and every answer shows the statutory text verbatim beneath it (see D14).
  3. The corpus is treated as the critical asset. The earlier ingestion script had never produced output: four of its six act identifiers pointed at unrelated laws, and its URL pattern did not exist. The corpus was rebuilt from verified identifiers. Because the official site was offline, it was fetched from Wayback Machine snapshots, with the source of every section recorded (see D9–D12).

2Objectives, constraints and scope

2.1 Objectives

  1. Answer rights-related questions in Bangla, English and Banglish, with a citation to the governing Act and section for every legal claim.
  2. Run every model on the presenter's MacBook so that the privacy claim ("your question never leaves this machine") is literally true during the demonstration.
  3. Demonstrate the system live from an iPhone, including what it does not do: refusing to give personal legal advice, and redirecting emergencies to 999 and the national legal-aid hotline 16430.

2.2 Constraints

HardwareApple M4, 16 GB unified memory, fanless; 239 GB free diskA model of about 8B parameters at 4-bit quantisation is the practical upper bound once macOS, the embedding model and development servers are resident. Sustained load causes thermal throttling.

2.3 Out of scope for this iteration

Fine-tuning; on-phone inference; voice input; acts outside the P0/P1 list in Section 6; production deployment, authentication and multi-user concerns.

6Knowledge-base construction procedure

The answer quality of a retrieval-augmented system cannot exceed the quality of its corpus. A missing section cannot be retrieved, and a wrong section will be cited with confidence. The corpus was therefore built and verified before any retrieval code was written.

6.3 Legal currency findings

Reading the index showed that several laws named in the earlier plan have changed. A system that cited them as written would give users outdated law.

  • Cyber Security Act, 2023 is repealed. It was replaced, through ordinances in 2025, by the সাইবার সুরক্ষা আইন, ২০২৬ (Cyber Protection Act, 2026). Only the 2026 Act is in scope.
  • The Code of Criminal Procedure was amended in 2026 (Act No. XI of 2026, effective 10 August 2025). Section 54 (arrest without warrant) was substituted, and sections 46A–46E (arrest procedure and duties of the arresting officer) and 54A (duty to state the reason for arrest) were inserted. The snapshot used already consolidates these changes, which was verified from the amendment notes attached to each section.
  • The Legal Aid Act, 2000 was amended in 2026, and the Women and Children Repression Prevention Act, 2000 was amended in 2020 and again in 2026. Whether the snapshots consolidate these amendments is checked in the review (Section 8).
Why this matters for the demonstration

The 2026 arrest-procedure reforms are exactly what a person stopped by the police needs to know. Under section 46A the arresting officer must carry visible identification, show an identity card on demand, and prepare a witnessed memorandum of arrest. Questions on these provisions will anchor the live demonstration.

7Retrieval and answer-generation pipeline

A 7–8B model is fluent but does not reliably know Bangladeshi law. The pipeline is designed so that the model never has to remember the law, only explain text placed in front of it, and so that a reader can verify every claim against that text.

7.1 Why each stage exists

StagePurposeFailure it prevents
Emergency pre-checkKeyword rules (e.g. "beating me", মারছে, "taken away now") return 999 and 16430 instantly.A person in danger waiting for a slow model to answer.
Verified answer cardsFourteen high-risk topics (arrest, detention, lawyer, search, torture, custodial death, confession, medical examination, RTI requests and appeals) are answered from text reviewed against the statute in advance. A card is used only when the question is semantically close (cosine ≥ 0.85) and mentions the card's topic words.Model error on exactly the questions the audience is most likely to ask.
Query rewriteEnglish and romanised Bangla questions are rewritten as a Bangla sentence (most post-1990 acts exist only in Bangla). For Banglish, the answer cards are tried again on the rewrite.Banglish queries matching no keywords; follow-up questions ("what about women?") losing context.
Hybrid retrieval + RRFDense vectors capture meaning across languages; BM25 captures exact terms and section numbers. Rank fusion combines them without score calibration.Either method alone missing a relevant section.
Bilingual contextWhere both language versions exist (currently the Constitution), both are given to the model.The 8B model misreading Bangla legal register it has seen less often.
Citation validationEvery section the model cites must be among the retrieved sections; otherwise the answer is withheld.Invented or mis-numbered citations, the most dangerous failure in legal Q&A.
Verbatim statute displayThe exact provision text is shown under every answer.Users relying on a paraphrase that subtly changes the law.

8Corpus review method

No legal professional is available to review the corpus before the presentation, so review is performed by the AI assistant using checks that are mechanical and repeatable wherever possible. This is a limitation and is stated as one. The review checks that the corpus faithfully reproduces the official text. It does not certify that any answer is legally correct for a particular person's situation.

  1. Completeness: parsed section count against the number of rows on the source page; investigate every gap. Applied: this found that bdlaws splits sub-sections into separate rows. The first parser silently dropped 84 such rows from the CrPC, including the end of section 54. Fixed by merging them into the preceding section.
  2. Script handling: Bangla section numbers terminate with either । (U+0964) or the visually identical ৷ (U+09F7). Applied: accepting both raised the RTI Act from 8 to 37 sections and the ICT Act from 1 to 85.
  3. Language parity: where both versions exist, English and Bangla section labels must match one-to-one. Applied: Constitution, 167 = 167 after the fixes above.
  4. Key-provision spot checks: the provisions most likely to be cited (Constitution Arts. 27, 31–35; CrPC §§46A–46E, 54, 54A, 61, 167; RTI §§4, 8, 9; Torture and Custodial Death Act) are read in full against the source page.
  5. Currency: the repeal or amendment status of each act is checked against the index, and the amendment notes of each key section are inspected.
  6. Residue: scan for footnote text that leaked into the statute body, navigation text, or empty sections.

10Demonstration setup

Network

The MacBook joins the iPhone's Personal Hotspot. Venue Wi-Fi often isolates clients from one another, which would prevent the phone reaching the laptop. A hotspot avoids this and also shows that no outside network is needed. The app is configured with the laptop's address on the hotspot network.

Client

Expo Go on the iPhone loads the app from Metro on the laptop. Before the presentation, it will be confirmed that the App Store version of Expo Go supports the project's SDK (54).

Laptop state

  • On mains power, because a fanless chassis throttles more on battery.
  • Models pre-loaded with a long keep-alive, so the first question is not slowed by loading.
  • Other heavy applications closed to protect the memory headroom (Figure 2).

Fallback

A screen recording of the full scripted demonstration, captured on Day 5, is kept on both devices.
